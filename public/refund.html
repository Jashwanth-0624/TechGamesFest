Cancellation & Refund Policy

Official financial protocol covering payment state transitions, slot occupancy reserves, catering commitments, and reversal conditions.

Organization: <code>sanket; (Dept. CSE - AI & DS, DSU) • Applies To: All Registered Squads • Event: Tech Games Fest 2026
🔒

Strict 100% Non-Refundable Policy on Successful Payment

Due to immediate resource allocations, strict room intake caps, real-time tournament slot locking, and upfront catering vendor contracts for the selected snack packages, all registration fees are 100% non-refundable once the transaction switches to a SUCCESS / PAID state.

01

Rationale Behind Slot & Catering Non-Refundability

Each of our seven tournament tracks carries a fixed intake maximum (e.g., exactly 30 squad slots per arena). When a team completes checkout via Cashfree:

  • Instant Capacity Reservation: Our database engine atomically reduces the available slots across your chosen competitions. Other aspiring teams are locked out from claiming those seats.
  • Direct Catering Procurement: Individual snack pack choices (Pack 1 @ ₹135 or Pack 2 @ ₹240) are dispatched directly into day-wise perishable catering inventory orders for Juice, Ice-Cream, Chips, Biscuits, and Diet Coke batches.
  • Tournament Brackets Lock: Match fixtures, Mentimeter quiz seat allocations, debate motions, and stage charade slots are finalized based on the locked roster.

Consequently, voluntary participant cancellations, team member no-shows, class timetable conflicts, or squad disbandment cannot be entertained for fee reversals.

02

Management Event Cancellation Exception (100% Payout)

Full University Cancellation Warranty: In the improbable event that Tech Games Fest 2026 is entirely cancelled, revoked, or indefinitely postponed by Dayananda Sagar University management or the School of Engineering administration due to unforeseen university mandates or force majeure, 100% of the aggregate collected registration fee will be refunded back to the original source bank accounts.

In such circumstances, automated refund payouts will be triggered via the Cashfree Merchant API directly back to the paying UPI ID, card, or netbanking account within 5 to 7 working banking days without any convenience deductions.

03

Technical Errors, Duplicate Deductions & Webhook Delays

In rare cases where network disruptions occur between bank UPI switches and Cashfree gateways:

Scenario Underlying Cause Resolution Protocol
Money Debited, Order Shows Pending Bank observer webhook delayed by UPI switch. Enter your 12-digit UPI UTR in the checkout safety box to self-verify, or wait up to 15 mins for automated polling reconciliation.
Duplicate Payment Deducted Participant initiated two sessions accidentally. Notify support within 48 hours with both Cashfree Order IDs and bank UTR numbers. Once verified, the secondary duplicate payment is refunded 100%.
Session Timed Out (180s Limit) Participant completed payment after timer expired. Cashfree automated refund engine will return the amount to the originating bank account within 3 to 5 banking days.
