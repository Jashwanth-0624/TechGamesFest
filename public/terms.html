Tournament Track Framework & Scope of Registration

Welcome to Tech Games Fest 2026 ("Fest"), organized and hosted by <code>sanket;, the official department club under the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Data Science), School of Engineering (SOE), Dayananda Sagar University (DSU), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

Registration covers guaranteed team enrollment in minimum two (2) and maximum three (3) designated tournament competition arenas across the 2-day festival schedule (16th & 17th November 2026) taking place at SOE: BLOCK A, LH:2, Dayananda Sagar University.

02

Mandatory Team Structure & Student Eligibility

  • Compulsory Trio Rule: Every registering squad must comprise exactly three (3) bona fide collegiate students. Single-player registrations or duos will not be allotted tournament access.
  • University Credential Verification: All participants must furnish a valid, legitimate 10-to-11 character University Seat Number (USN) (e.g., ENG24DS0018 or 1DS23AI001) and an authorized university/collegiate email address.
  • Identity Physical Verification: All three registered members must carry and produce their physical college RFID Smart Identity Cards during morning on-ground check-in at SOE Block A LH:2. Impersonation will result in immediate disqualification of the entire team and referral to university disciplinary committees.

04

Rules of Sportsmanship, Anti-Cheating & AI Disclaimers

Every participant must abide by fair-play principles and event-specific regulations:

  • Zero-AI Enforcement (Product Pitching): For Pitch Perfect, proposed products and inventions must operate purely without artificial intelligence. AI is permitted solely for slide-deck visuals (max 4 slides).
  • Non-Verbal Guidelines (Stage Charades): For Mudra Mime, any spoken word, lip-syncing, air-writing, or room interference will forfeit points directly to the acting squad.
  • Speed Quiz Discipline: For TechGyaan, exactly ONE mobile device per team is allowed on the Mentimeter digital console. Split-screening, background browser lookup, or external communication triggers automated ban.
  • Handcrafted Mediums: For Collage Chronicles, scissor cutting is strictly forbidden. All tearing and shaping must be executed exclusively by hand.
  • Judge & Convener Finality: Scores tallied by official faculty evaluators and student conveners are absolute and final. Disputes or disrespectful behavior on stage or campus will cause immediate badge revocation.

Secure Digital Payment Processing
